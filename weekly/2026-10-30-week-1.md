# Week 1 pack: Instagram + YouTube (Oct 30 to Nov 3)

Schedule each video for **6 pm Pacific** on its day. TikTok posts by itself through Metricool, so this is only Instagram and YouTube.

## How to do it on your phone

1. **Save the video:** tap the video link, then the Share button, then **Save Video** so it lands in Photos.
2. **Instagram:** tap **+**, pick **Reel**, choose the video, tap **Next** twice, paste the caption, then open **More options** (or **Advanced settings**) and turn on **Schedule**. Pick the date and 6:00 PM, then tap **Schedule reel**.
3. **YouTube:** in the YouTube Studio app (or the YouTube app's **+**, then **Upload a video**), pick the video, paste the title and description, choose **No, it's not made for kids**, set visibility to **Schedule** for the same day at 6:00 PM, and upload. If your phone doesn't offer Schedule, do this step at studio.youtube.com on a computer.

Tip: on a computer every box below has a copy button in its corner; on a phone, press and hold the text to select it.
